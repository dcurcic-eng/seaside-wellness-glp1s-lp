Your 3-month medical weight management program, weekly injectable support when clinically appropriate, provider-guided follow-ups and progress monitoring, and six EMTONE® sessions per treatment area.

EMTONE® is a non-invasive treatment that combines radiofrequency, therapeutic heat and targeted massage. It helps improve skin laxity, support collagen production and reduce the look of cellulite and rippling on areas like the abdomen, arms, thighs and buttocks.

You may be able to continue the program. Your provider will talk through your options based on your goals and progress.
